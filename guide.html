How to use the Clinical AI Course

Every chapter works the same way. Learn it once here, then use it for any chapter. Lessons take about 2 minutes each, and you can split a chapter into short sittings.

STEP 1

Pick a chapter

On the course home page, click any chapter card that shows a green Start label. Cards marked Coming soon are not open yet.

Once you begin, the card shows a progress bar, your learning index and how many lessons you have finished.

STEP 2

Set your plan

  1. Answer three quick questions. There is no pass mark. They only set the right pace for you.
  2. Choose your time per session: 5, 10, or 20 minutes or more.
  3. Choose your learning style: Listen and watch (Sam reads aloud) or Read at my own pace.
  4. Click Make my plan. The course splits the chapter into sittings that fit your time.
If you already know the topic, the plan may offer the Fast track, which takes you more quickly to the cases.

STEP 3

Take a short lesson

Each lesson is a few slides with Sam, your virtual tutor, and lasts about 2 minutes.

  • Use ◀ Back and Next ▶ to move between slides.
  • Captions show Sam's words, so you can follow without sound.
  • Click Explain more simply for a plain-English summary.
  • Already know it? Click I know this: skip to the check.
No sound? Check your volume, or choose a different voice from the voice menu. You can also switch to Read mode at any time.

STEP 4

Answer the quick check

  1. Before answering, say whether you are sure or not sure. This is optional. It feeds your self-awareness score.
  2. Pick an answer. You see straight away whether it is right, with a short explanation.
  3. Wrong first time? You get a second chance: a different question on the same idea.
Missed both? The lesson is marked for review. Try it again in Read mode. Practising again is not scored.

STEP 5

Work through the clinical case

Each lesson ends with a short real-world situation, such as a practice meeting or a patient in clinic. You choose what to do at each step.

  • Good call earns 2 points.
  • Partly earns 1 point.
  • Not the best choice earns 0 points.

Read the feedback after each choice. It explains the reasoning, which is the real learning. You can retry the case.

STEP 6

Take the final case test

When you have finished all the lessons, take the final test: 12 to 14 questions covering the whole chapter. Each question tells you which lesson it comes from.

Afterwards you get a personal learning path. It tells you which lessons to revisit and why, for example "you missed the quick check" or "2 final-test questions wrong".

You can take the test early, but you will usually score higher after finishing the lessons. You can retake it, and your last attempt is kept.

STEP 7

Read your learning index and get your certificate

Your learning index (0 to 100) combines four things:

  • Knowledge (35%): quick checks right first time, plus final-test answers
  • Applying it (35%): points from the clinical cases
  • Self-awareness (15%): how often your "sure" or "not sure" matched your answer
  • Completion (15%): lessons finished and final test taken

Once you have taken the final test and reached Competent (60 or more), type your name and click Download certificate. It saves as a picture.
